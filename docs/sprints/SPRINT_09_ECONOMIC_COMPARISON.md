# Sprint 09 — Intervention Cost Modelling and Economic Comparison

## Outcome

Add factual recurring-cost evidence to existing paired operational comparison and sensitivity. Every monetary input is explicit, user-supplied, finite, non-negative, and denominated in one three-letter currency for one model time unit.

## Scope

- Pure per-run cost snapshots from summary operational results.
- Online aggregation and paired common-random-number cost deltas.
- Optional, separate one-time intervention costs with explicit amortization only.
- Operational-objective/cost trade-off labels, economic guardrails, and incremental recurring cost per observed improvement.
- A 0.6.0 sensitivity wrapper that captures economics during the same sweep.
- Strict 0.7.0 schemas, API, CLI, bounded Scenario Lab UI, exports, integrity checks, and serial benchmarks.

## Non-goals

No revenue, profit, ROI, recommendations, optimization, inferred costs, multi-period finance, persistence, authentication, deployment, or operational-ranking changes.

## Done means

GM-100–GM-121 are implemented with honest executed/blocker status; ordinary runs retain zero events; economics adds no simulation work units; and all returned language remains descriptive rather than prescriptive.
