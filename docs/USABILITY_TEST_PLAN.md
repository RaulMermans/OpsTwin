# Usability Test Plan

## Persona and protocol

Test with first-time operations managers, support leads, business analysts, process-improvement specialists, or general business users. Participants understand staffing, queues, and SLAs but not simulation terminology. Do not introduce the product before Task 1. Use think-aloud; offer help only after two minutes blocked. Do not collect sensitive personal data.

## Tasks

1. In one sentence, say what decision the product helps make.
2. Compare adding one Level 1 agent with making triage 25% faster.
3. Identify the larger observed change in average cycle time and explain how certain the evidence is.
4. Find where the workflow differed.
5. Explain whether playback represents every simulated run or one selected run.
6. Find where an additional agent's cost would be entered.

## Observation worksheet

For each task record completion (success/partial/failure), first click, elapsed time, incorrect actions, assistance, terminology questions, interpretation mistakes, participant quote, Single Ease Question (1–7), and confidence (1–5). Record whether technical settings were edited unnecessarily for Task 2.

## Targets

90% correct product explanation, guided run without assistance, primary-result discovery, and direction interpretation; 80% playback understanding; median guided run below three minutes; zero critical interpretation errors.

## Evidence classification

Automated checks and expert/simulated review are not human participant evidence. If five participants are unavailable, label the product-owner test and two independent first-time reviews separately.
